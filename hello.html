🌿 Growing Tips

  • Choose the right soil: Loamy, well-drained soil is best for most vegetables.
  • Sunlight: Most vegetables need 6–8 hours of direct sunlight daily.
  • Spacing: Avoid overcrowding to reduce disease and improve airflow.
  • Mulching: Helps retain moisture and suppress weeds.

💧 Water Requirements by Vegetable

Vegetable Water Needed (per week) Notes
Tomatoes 1.5–2 inches Deep watering encourages root growth
Carrots 1 inch Keep soil consistently moist
Lettuce 1.5 inches Shallow roots need frequent watering
Peppers 1–1.5 inches Water deeply but infrequently
Cucumbers 2 inches High water demand during fruiting
